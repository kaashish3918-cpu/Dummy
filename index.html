Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What types of perfumes are available at Aromaxx?

Aromaxx offers a variety of premium fragrances including fresh, floral, woody, spicy and long-lasting perfumes for different preferences and occasions.

How long does an Aromaxx perfume last?

Perfume longevity depends on the fragrance composition, skin type and environment. Different fragrances may have different lasting durations.

How can I choose the right perfume?

You can choose a perfume based on your preferred fragrance notes, personality, occasion and whether you prefer fresh, floral, woody or warm scents.

How should I store my perfume?

Store your perfume in a cool and dry place away from direct sunlight and excessive heat to help preserve its fragrance.

Can I wear Aromaxx perfume every day?

Yes. You can select a fragrance according to your personal preference and the occasion, including daily use.
